Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Who runs this site: Chess Puerto Rico Coffee, sponsored by PuertoRicoCoffeeShop.com. Contact: system@chesspuertoricocoffee.com. Our server is in the United States, hosted by IONOS. If you visit from another country, your data is processed in the United States.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Chess Puerto Rico Coffee, we may collect:

  • Account Information: Username and email address. An email address is required to create an account. We use it only for account emails (a welcome message, confirming your account, password resets, log-in links you ask for, confirming a change of email address, and reopening a closed account). It is never shown to other players.
  • Game Data: Chess games played, moves, results, ratings
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, browser type
  • Contact Form Data: Name, email, and message content when you contact us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the chess platform
  • Track game history and maintain ratings
  • Detect and prevent cheating
  • Respond to your inquiries via the contact form

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes. Game data (moves, results) may be publicly visible as part of the chess platform's functionality.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your login session and preferences. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Embedded YouTube videos are covered in section 9.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption and secure password storage to protect your data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your game data

If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have rights under the GDPR (the EU GDPR or the UK GDPR, whichever applies to you), including the right to object to how we use your data and the right to complain to your local data protection authority. To use any of your rights, contact us at system@chesspuertoricocoffee.com.

7. Children's Privacy

You must be 13 or older, or older if the law in your country requires it. The sign-up form asks you to confirm that you are 13 or older, and no account is created without that confirmation. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, email us at system@chesspuertoricocoffee.com. If we confirm it, we will delete the account (section 8 explains what deleting an account removes).

8. How Long We Keep Your Data

  • While your account is open: we keep your account information, games and settings.
  • If you close your account (in your account settings): your profile is hidden and you are logged out everywhere. Your games and forum posts stay on the site under your username, your blog posts are no longer listed, and we keep your email address so that you can reopen the account later if you wish.
  • If you delete your account (at chesspuertoricocoffee.com/account/delete): this cannot be undone. Your email address, profile, private studies, blog posts, forum posts, games against the computer and imported games are deleted. Your other games, public studies and tournament results stay on the site without your username, because they are part of other players' records. Your username stays reserved and cannot be registered again, and teams and classes you created stay unless you close them first.
  • Contact form messages: kept in our email only as long as we need them to answer you.
  • Backups: our nightly database backups are kept for 14 days and our full server backups for up to about three months. They are used only to restore the site after a failure. After an account is deleted, its data disappears from the backups as they expire.
  • Server logs: our web server records requests (for example the IP address and the page requested) for security and to fix problems. These logs are rotated automatically by size and deleted after a limited time.

9. Embedded YouTube Videos

Our video pages play videos hosted on YouTube, a Google service. When you open a video page, the YouTube player is loaded in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (from youtube-nocookie.com), and the video library pages show thumbnail images loaded from YouTube's image servers. When the player or thumbnails load, YouTube receives technical information such as your IP address, and when you play a video YouTube may collect information and store cookies in your browser. That processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not this one. The video section on our homepage uses copies of the thumbnails stored on our own server, so the homepage itself loads nothing from YouTube.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the platform.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us or email system@chesspuertoricocoffee.com.
